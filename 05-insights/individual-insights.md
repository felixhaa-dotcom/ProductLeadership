# Individual Insights: Strategy Development Process

> Module 6 · Leverage AI Tools for Leadership Execution
>
> Your personal reflection on building the strategy end to end.

## Friction points

_What was the hardest part of building this strategy, and how did you work through it?_

> The hardest part was holding the strategic no when commercially attractive requests competed with the field-adoption bet. The work became much clearer once I treated “not now” as part of the strategy rather than as a prioritization failure.

## Key learnings

_A few surprising discoveries or insights you gained from the course overall._

> I will trace major roadmap items back to explicit outcomes, separate stakeholder urgency from strategic importance, and define the kill criterion before committing significant capacity.

## "Aha!" moment

_Your main "aha" moment during the project process._

> The biggest shift was realizing that a business case is not complete when the upside looks attractive; it is complete when I have also defined what evidence would make me stop funding it.
